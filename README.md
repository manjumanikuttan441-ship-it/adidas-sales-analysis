# adidas-sales-analysis
Over view --> This project is a comprehensive analysis of Adidas sales data formed to analyze the sales trends and productive features.
I used this project to demonstrate my ability to transform row data into actionable business insights and build an interactive Power Bi dashboard that supports strategic decision making.
3 main pages are there--> Executive Dashboard,Product performance dashboard,Insights and recommendations
insights and recommendations: 
Product Performance: Men's street footwear and women's apparel dominate total sales volume and gross profitability.
Sales Channels: In-store outlet and physical retail purchases yield higher returns than digital e-commerce channels.
Geographic Trends: The West and Southeast regions generate over half of total net sales.
Temporal Patterns: Sales activity shows strong seasonal spikes peaking in the third quarter
Strategic RecommendationsInventory Allocation: Shift stock focus toward high-demand street footwear and women's lines ahead
Digital Growth: Invest in targeted online marketing and user experience upgrades to capture untapped e-commerce potential.
Regional Campaigns: Scale up promotional campaigns in top-performing regions while optimizing logistics for underperforming zones
