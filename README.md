                                                Over view

The Adidas Sales Data Analysis project focuses on examining Adidas sales information to understand overall business performance. The dataset contains details such as retailers, products, regions, cities, sales, units sold, price per unit, operating profit, and operating margin.

The project involves cleaning, transforming, and analyzing the raw data using Microsoft Excel. Excel tools such as formulas, filters, PivotTables, and charts are used to identify important sales patterns and trends.

The analysis helps determine top-performing products, leading retailers, high-sales regions, profitability, and changes in sales over time. The final results provide useful insights that can support better business decisions, improve sales performance, and identify opportunities for future growth.








                                           Project Objectives of Adidas Sales Data







To analyze overall sales performance of Adidas products across different regions and retailers.

To identify top-performing products based on total sales, units sold, and revenue generated.

To evaluate retailer performance and determine which retailers contribute the most to Adidas sales.

To analyze regional sales trends by comparing sales across different regions, states, and cities.

To examine profitability by analyzing operating profit and operating margin.

To identify sales trends over time using invoice dates, months, quarters, and years.

To clean and transform the raw sales data in Excel by removing duplicates, handling missing values, and standardizing formats.

To create meaningful reports and visualizations using Excel charts, PivotTables, and dashboards.

To support data-driven decision-making by identifying areas of strong performance and opportunities for improvement.

To provide useful business insights that can help improve sales, profitability, product planning, and overall business performance.





                                                     Key Analysis of Adidas Sales Data




The key analysis of Adidas sales data focuses on understanding sales performance, profitability, product demand, retailer contribution, and regional trends.

Total Sales Analysis


Analyze total revenue generated by Adidas and identify periods with the highest and lowest sales.

Product Performance Analysis

Compare different product categories and individual products to identify the best-selling and least-performing products.

Retailer Analysis

Evaluate sales generated through different retailers and identify the retailers contributing the most to overall revenue.

Regional Sales Analysis

Compare sales across regions, states, and cities to determine the strongest and weakest markets.

Profitability Analysis

Analyze operating profit and operating margin to identify products, retailers, and regions that generate higher profits.

Units Sold Analysis

Examine the number of units sold to understand product demand and customer purchasing patterns.

Price Analysis

Compare price per unit with units sold and total sales to understand how pricing affects sales performance.

Time-Based Sales Analysis

Analyze sales by month, quarter, and year to identify trends, growth patterns, and seasonal changes.

Category Analysis

Compare footwear, apparel, and accessories to determine which category performs best in terms of sales and profit.

Performance Comparison

Use Excel PivotTables, charts, and dashboards to compare key performance indicators and generate actionable business insights.
